Dynamic programming is careful recursion with memory. First find the recurrence: express the answer for a state in terms of smaller states (the choices you can make). Then remember each sub-answer so you never recompute it — top-down with a memo, or bottom-up by filling a table. Coin Change is the canonical 1-D DP: `dp[a]` = fewest coins to make amount `a` = 1 + min over coins of `dp[a - coin]`.
