A prefix sum array `pre` where `pre[i]` is the sum of the first `i` elements lets you get the sum of any range `[l, r)` in O(1) as `pre[r] - pre[l]`. Combine it with a hash map of previously-seen prefix sums and you can count subarrays with a given sum in a single pass: at index `i`, the number of subarrays ending here that sum to `k` equals how many times `pre[i] - k` has appeared.
