AB11 · Degradation badges

v2 has no alerting (D34): nothing pages the owner when grading degrades. These in-app badges are how the owner — and the tester cohort — notice it. Four badges, each an icon and words, each opening a popover that says what still works: Grading pending · self-report, Grader busy, Auto-grading off, and the M4 placeholder xLearn AI paused. They appear on Problems, in the Workspace (AB07 / AB08) and on Today, only when judge is configured and the viewer is in the cohort. They never expose internals: no pack version, token, error string or host detail.

Board-wide rules D34 · ADR-0034 §2 · m3-09 task 7

One source. GET /api/judge/status[?path=dsa] → {state: ok | saturated | breaker | off, breakerLevel?, evaluable, items?{<id>: evaluable | contract_mismatch | unsupported | invalid | no_pack}}, cached 15 s at the gateway. The SPA reads it on page load, every 30 s while a judge surface is open, and right after any 429 or 503. Mapping. An item not evaluable (any of the four other values) → Grading pending · self-report on that item only · saturated or breaker → Grader busy · off (L15 admission closed, or judge unreachable) → Auto-grading off · evaluable = 0 → a page banner on Problems and Today. xLearn AI paused is an M4 placeholder with no M3 source.
Presence by config (ADR-0034 §2). Badges render only when judge is configured and the viewer is in the owner/tester cohort (before GA). Otherwise there is no judge on the page and nothing to badge (F6). Never internals. No pack version, contract hash, PAT, error string, queue depth, host or pod name. The popover says what still works, in plain words. A11y. Every badge is icon + text (never colour alone), text ≥ 4.5:1 on its tint, and it never sits beside a same-coloured difficulty chip without its label. The badge is a button (aria-expanded) inside a role="status" wrapper, so its arrival is announced once, politely. No toasts, nothing steals focus.

Behaviour

  • Click / Enter / Space opens the popover under the badge; Esc, a click outside or the badge again closes it. It is non-modal and holds text only: focus stays on the badge, and the same text is the badge's accessible description.
  • At most one popover open at a time. Popovers never open on their own.

Tone and contrast

  • info (blue) = your grade is still yours to give · warn (amber) = slower, still working · err (red) = auto-grading unavailable · muted = placeholder. Badge text on its tint, over the page surfaces: info ≥ 6.3:1, warn ≥ 8.2:1, err ≥ 5.5:1; muted uses --ds-dim on inset (7.4:1).
  • Badge text always carries the state; the icon is decorative (aria-hidden).

Decision

  • D34: no alerting in v2, so the in-app badge is the signal (ADR-0035 §3). Data from m3-09's status route; the learner-facing name for spec_mismatch is contract_mismatch.
  • "xLearn AI paused" is drawn now so M4 slots into the same set; it is labelled a placeholder and has no M3 behaviour.

  • When the status read clears a state, the badge disappears and one polite line is announced ("The grader is back to normal." / "Auto-grading is back on."). No toasts and nothing takes focus. The page banner (F2b, F4) goes with it.
  • An attempt that started in self mode stays self-graded (its grading_mode was pinned at start); only new attempts pick up the judge again. Its HUD keeps "Grading pending · self-report".
  • A badge appearing is announced once by its role="status" wrapper (the badge text). Flapping is damped by the 15 s cache: a state must hold across two reads before the badge changes.
