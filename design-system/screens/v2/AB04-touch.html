AB04 ★ · A8 Touch

A revision touch becomes a server-timed attempt (practice purpose=touch). DSA runs three steps under one 20:00 clock: name the pattern (a lock-in that counts under 2:00), re-solve from a blank editor (self-attested until the judge arrives in M3), and state the complexity (a lock-in). Correctness stays hidden until the touch concludes; the result reads "advances to Day 21" or "resets to Day 1". Levels 4–5 run under mock conditions. The clock never pauses, and there is no re-implement step.

Board-wide rules D15 · D16 · D27 · t4 §3.6–§3.7 · m2-01 task 4

Two clocks, one server start. The main clock counts down the band timer (DSA 20:00) from the server start to the server deadline. The pattern clock counts up from the same start, with a mark at 2:00. Both render from startedAt / deadline; there is no client timer state and no pause (D15). One live touch per problem, at the lowest due level. Starting again while one is open resumes it (F13). The touch concludes the moment every criterion is decided (all three steps locked or attested), on End review, or at the deadline + 5 s. A touch that was never shown is voided silently: no frame exists for it, because the learner never saw it.
Withheld until the result: the pattern chip, any ✓ / ✗, and the accepted answers. Before conclusion a decided step shows only a neutral lock and its time ("Locked at 0:48"), never a colour or a mark. Keyboard: Enter locks in the focused step · Esc closes a modal · End review is never the default action (it is never focused first and never bound to Enter). Screen readers: clocks are role="timer" with aria-live="off"; one hidden aria-live="polite" region announces "5 minutes left" and "1 minute left" (main) and "1 minute to lock in the pattern" (pattern, at 1:00 elapsed). Nothing ticks aloud.
