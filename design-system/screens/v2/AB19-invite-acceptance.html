Welcome to xLearn

Checking your invite…
  • On mount the SPA matches #invite=([A-Za-z0-9_-]{22}), keeps the code in sessionStorage["xl_invite"] (in-memory fallback when storage is blocked) and always calls history.replaceState for any #invite= fragment, valid-looking or not. The fragment never reaches Traefik logs or a Referer.
  • Then POST /api/invites/check {code} → {valid, expires_at} only (L2: 5/min per IP). Valid → F3; not valid → F4.
  • If the check itself fails (429, 5xx, network) the banner reads "Couldn't check your invite — try again." with [Try again]; the invite is never shown as invalid for that, and Sign up stays hidden until a check succeeds.
  • aria-live="polite" announces "Checking your invite…"; the form skeleton is aria-hidden. The spinner stops under prefers-reduced-motion (the text stays).

Gate

  • Shown when /api/me → acceptance: {required: true, mode: "first", notice_version: "privacy-notice@1", region, consents}. It runs before the path step for every account, once. Until it's done the gateway answers 403 acceptance_required on every non-onboarding API, and any such 403 mid-session routes the SPA back here.
  • Full screen, in the auth layout like steps 1–4 (the .xl-auth__card column). The stepper joins today's four steps: this is Step 1 of 5; path, budget, username and coach become 2–5.

Items

  • Required: 18+ (an attestation, not verification — ADR-0033 Consequences); the notice at its version (the link opens AB20 in a new tab; version and date come from the one constant the server checks, lib/notice.ts); the region, pre-filled from the invite (account.region, India when none) and confirmed from the full ISO 3166-1 list (ADR-0032's EU/EEA voice gate reads it).
  • Optional, both unticked: two separate consents, never bundled with the required items. The second is disabled until the first is ticked; unticking the first unticks and disables the second (withdrawing graded withdraws passing, m4-05 SetConsents). Withdrawable any time in Settings (AB18). Off → the learner grades their own work.

Submit

  • Continue → POST /api/onboarding/step {"step":"accept", "notice_version", "agree_notice": true, "age_18": true, "region", "consents": {"ai_review_graded", "ai_review_passing"}}. Recorded with the notice version in account_consent (privacy_notice, age_18, the AI kinds) plus accepted_at; then Step 2 of 5.
  • Sign out records nothing. There is no erase entry here: someone who declines signs out and asks the owner, at the notice's contact address, for a CLI erase (l-05 task 3).
  • The consent group lands with l-05 in v1.17.0, after M4, when M4's consent kinds exist.

a11y
