/xlearn/dsa/problems — the course's full item list, now with two independent done markers: the Course marker (counted attempts: graded, scheduled for revision) and the Arena marker (free practice that never counts, D10). Board-wide rules D10 · ADR-0027 §1 · ADR-0034 §2

Two markers, two sources, never mixed (D10). The Course marker comes from practice's per-item state (counted work only: status, attemptState, deadlineAt, lastOutcome{grade, gradedBy, trust}, the due touch). The Arena marker comes from judge's arena_progress through one batched GET /api/paths/dsa/arena/progress (firstPassedAt → "Solved in arena · Oct 12"; source=manual → "Studied · Oct 10"), both via the BFF (m3-09). Arena activity never moves a course number: not the course marker, not the week rollup (AB12-W4), not the summary's "done in the course" count, not Progress. Grades carry words and an icon (Clean ✓ green · Rough amber · Assisted blue · Miss ⚑ red, AB07's outlook tones) plus a provenance mini-chip in AB06's vocabulary: judge ✓ passed on hidden tests · judge concluded by the judge without a pass (time limit or give-up) · self · honour self-reported.
Withheld while live (ADR-0027 §1, m1-06 lists rule). An item with an open counted attempt (including awaiting_evaluation and self_grade_pending) or a due or live touch shows Pattern hidden while live (or AB04's "… while due") instead of its pattern chip. Every other row keeps v1's pattern chip. No pattern filter, and search matches title and number only. Presence by config (ADR-0034 §2). The arena marker, the legend's Arena line, the "Arena done" filter, the grading chips and the summary's arena count exist only when arena/progress and /api/judge/status answer 200 (judge configured and the account in the owner/tester cohort). A 404 renders F7. Grading chip sits right after the difficulty chip (AB11-F2): Judge for items[id] = evaluable, otherwise AB11's row badge Self-report (contract_mismatch | unsupported | invalid | no_pack).
A11y on every frame. Text --ds-text 15:1 and --ds-dim 7.2:1 on the panels; --ds-muted only for non-essential meta; badge and chip text ≥ 5.5:1 on its tint. Every tone travels with a word or an icon, never colour alone. The drawn focus ring (bd-focus, the app's :focus-visible) marks where focus lands first in a frame; each frame's notes give its focus order. Status changes are aria-live="polite"; nothing steals focus; ds-spin becomes a static icon under reduced motion. Touch targets ≥ 24 px with 8 px spacing (WCAG 2.5.8); 44 px rows in the 390 px sheets.

Controls → URL

  • Status ds-seg (one value): All · Not started (status=new) · Course done (course: a concluded counted attempt) · Arena done (arena: arena_progress.done, solved or studied) · Review due (due: a touch due today or overdue).
  • Difficulty chips (multi, diff=easy,med,hard), grading chips (multi, grading=judge,self), week select (week=2), search (q=, title or # number). Every change replaces the query with history.replaceState, so Back leaves the page and a reload restores the view.
  • Filtering is client-side over the one list response; groups with no match are dropped; the week heading and its "Current" / "Ahead" chip stay on the ones that remain.

No pattern filter

  • A pattern (or concept) filter is left out on purpose: filtering by "Two Pointers" would sort a live item into its pattern even while its chip is withheld — a withhold leak (ADR-0027 §1). Search matches title and number, never pattern.
  • "Self-report" matches every item whose grading chip is AB11's row badge (not evaluable right now).

A11y · narrow

  • The seg is a radiogroup (arrow keys); chips are toggle buttons (aria-pressed) with a check icon when on — never colour alone. The result line is role="status" ("4 problems · Arena done · Medium") and announces politely after each change. Clear filters resets the query and returns focus to "All".
  • < 1024 px: one "Filters (2)" button opens a bottom sheet (F8-N2); search stays inline.
