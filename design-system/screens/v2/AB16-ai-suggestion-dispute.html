AB16 ★ · A3 AI suggestion / dispute

An AI-produced result is a suggestion, not a verdict (D14). The learner accepts it, edits it within the deterministic ceilings (a changed grade is labelled self-set), disputes it once for a blind re-grade that can go up or down, or — for an honor-grade probe on a touch — claims an accepted answer once. An untouched suggestion auto-accepts after 24 h from a server deadline, unless it is flagged. Manual entry is reachable from every AI state, and nothing ever waits on AI. Live in v2.0: F2, F3–F4, F13, F14, F15 (drawn first). Rubric items — contract built in M4, first used by a rubric course: F1, F5–F12, drawn on an illustrative rubric item, because no v2.0 course item carries an ai_rubric step.

Board-wide rules D14 · t4 §3.5 · t5 §7 item 5 · m4-04 · m4-06 task 1

Countdowns come from the server. Every "auto-accepts in …" renders from accept_deadline_at (the BFF serves acceptDeadlineAt) corrected by server_now, never from a client timer. At 0 the SPA refetches; it never concludes anything locally. A null deadline (flagged, or a dispute pending) shows no countdown and no "auto-accepts" text. Format: 10 h or more H:MM ("23:41"); 1–10 h H:MM:SS ("3:12:40"); under 1 h M:SS. The visible text stays compact; aria-label and title carry the long form ("auto-accepts in 23 hours 41 minutes · Thu, Dec 10, 14:05"). Screen readers: the countdown is role="timer" with aria-live="off". One aria-live="polite" region announces "1 hour left to review this grade" and "5 minutes left to review this grade", and nothing else.
Suggestions, not verdicts (D14). Accept, edit (bounded by the ceiling), dispute once, or leave it: 24 h later it is accepted as it stands. Submitting triggers the downstream actions: the schedule dates from anchor_at (the lock), and a mistake entry opens when one applies. Code verdicts are never editable; mistake category, notes and concepts always are. Manual paths: Change… (F2), Keep as not passed (F3), Edit… (F1, F7, F10), Set my own grade… (F8), Grade it myself (F11), manual entry (F12). Never shown: rubric anchors, must-cover lists, exemplars, hidden inputs, the dispute text after it is sent, or why a grade was flagged. Accepted probe answers appear only after the touch locks. Keyboard: Esc closes a modal; labelled or spending choices (Request re-grade, Set grade) are never the default button.
