AB21 · Erase account

The erase section of Settings: the last card of "Sign-in & security". An eligible tester sees what's erased, what stays for a while, and the 60-day username hold; erasing needs a session under 5 minutes old and a typed confirmation, and can't be undone — xLearn keeps no off-node backups. The owner is never erased on the web (no button), and learners see a tester-only note until the L exit.

Erase account

Permanent. Only you can start it, from a fresh sign-in.

Permanently erase your xLearn account and everything in it. This can't be undone — xLearn keeps no off-node backups.

What's erased
  • Sign-inemail, password, GitHub link, sessions
  • Profile and settingsname, username, time zone, study budget, reminders
  • Learningattempts, timers, outcomes, revision schedule, mistake journal, weak areas
  • Mocks and progressmock sessions, scores, progress, your public profile
  • Coachyour saved AI keys, coach chats
What stays for a while
  • A weekly server image kept by the host may hold your data for up to about 7 days, and a pre-release snapshot for up to 1 day. If one is ever restored, the owner re-runs the erases made since it was taken.
  • Some internal event records keep a random account id, and older sign-up records also keep your display name, until the owner removes them on request.
  • If xLearn AI was on, copies Anthropic holds expire on its schedule (up to 30 days).
Your username @asha is held for 60 days, then anyone can claim it.

Copy

  • "xLearn keeps no off-node backups" (D12) — "off-node" always stays in, because the host's weekly image exists.
  • What's erased follows ADR-0027 §6 across every owning service (R-AC5). What stays is the flagged wording (Decisions to confirm #6–#8): the weekly image (~7 days) and the pre-release snapshot (1 day, ADR-0034 §4.3), re-run after a restore (§4.2 step 3; no erase ledger, D12); the random account id and the display name in older account_created events until a re-seal on request; Anthropic's own retention (ADR-0031 §4). Never a promise that the leftovers are anonymous.
  • "Held for 60 days" is the username cooldown (P11, released_username). The Privacy notice link opens AB20 §6 (and is how AB20-F4 is reached from Settings).

Erase account

Permanent. Only you can start it, from a fresh sign-in.

Permanently erase your xLearn account and everything in it. This can't be undone — xLearn keeps no off-node backups.

For your security, sign in again to erase your account. You'll come straight back here.
  • When now ≥ session_fresh_until (session created_at + 5 min), or when DELETE /api/me answers 403 reauth_required (F3 then closes and the card switches to this). The What's erased / What stays lists stay as in F1 (not repeated here).
  • Sign in again signs out, then returns to Settings → Sign-in & security after login (?tab=account; no PII in the URL). The new session is fresh for 5 minutes → F1.
  • identity reads the session's own created_at; no client clock or header is trusted.
AB21-F7· Owner refused· role owner · ADR-0033 §7new

Erase account

Not available from the web for this account.

Owner

The owner account can't be erased from the web. Use the admin CLI: identity admin account erase.

  • erase_web: "owner_cli_only". No button, no modal, no link — so no XSS can wipe the owner. If DELETE /api/me is called anyway: 403 erase_owner_cli_only.
  • The CLI (identity admin account erase <user> --confirm <user>, via kubectl exec) is the only path for the owner and for suspended accounts (ADR-0033 §8).
  • Neutral styling (no danger colour): nothing here can be acted on.

Erasing from the web is open to tester accounts for now. To erase this account, contact the owner.
