AB18 · AI allowance meter + Settings AI consents

How much xLearn AI (included) the learner has left, and the three switches that govern it. The allowance is a percentage, never money (ADR-0031 §5). When it runs out, or xLearn AI pauses for everyone, grading still works and the learner picks mistakes and grades themselves (D14). The consents are the Settings twins of AB19's acceptance step — the same labels and the same retention sentence — plus a separate behavioral opt-in. All three are unticked until the learner chooses and free to switch off with no confirmation. Degraded states say what still works and never promise that anyone was told (D34).

Board-wide rules ADR-0031 §4–§5 · t5 §6 · t5 §8 · m4-05 task 4

The meter reads percentages only. GET /api/me/ai-allowance → {state: ok|low|paused|off, reason, used_pct, resets_at, day_used_pct, day_resets_at, analyses_left_today, finals_left_today, global_paused}. The API never returns money to the browser; the SPA's type admits no money field. No currency symbol or amount appears in any state. What drives what: state → F1 (ok), F2 (low, from the server, never a client threshold), F3/F4 (paused, by reason account_month / account_day); global_paused → F5; state=off, reason=ai_disabled → F6; off, no_consent → F8. Times come from the server in UTC and render in the account's time zone: "resets Jan 1" (month) and "back at 05:30 tomorrow" (day, 00:00 UTC in IST).
Consents (GET/PATCH /api/me/consents, kinds ai_review_graded, ai_review_passing, ai_behavioral, version ai-consent@1): real <button role="switch" aria-checked>, unticked for an account that never chose; the second is disabled until the first is on; turning the first off turns the second off. Effective for new work at once: after each change the gateway refreshes judge's account cache. Nothing already sent is recalled; copies at the provider expire on its own schedule (up to 30 days). Same words as AB19: the two consent labels and the retention sentence are AB19-F9's, verbatim. Helper lines, the behavioral toggle and the "Last changed" line are Settings-only.
